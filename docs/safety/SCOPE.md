# Safety and scope

PolityBench is a **research environment**.

## Allowed agent controls

Budgets, taxes (propose), executive allocations, health/education/social funding, infrastructure priorities, lawful emergency response, regulation proposals, strategic diplomacy/trade posture, anti-corruption funding, environmental/energy policy, factual public-risk communication.

## Hard exclusions

- Tactical military operations / weapon targeting
- Operational cyberattack mechanics
- Individualized surveillance / real-person dossiers
- Election manipulation / voter suppression
- Targeted political persuasion / protected-group propaganda optimization
- Direct control of courts, independent media, or (by default) the central bank

## Information environment

Misinformation pressure may exist in the world model. Government actions are limited to disclosure, public-service communication, fact-checking support, and media literacy — never covert manipulation.

## Interpretation ban

Do not present PolityBench scores as evidence that an AI should govern a real country.
